/ 06

Questions, answered straight.

Is Talk really free?

Yes — 15 minutes of calling every day, free. Free is not forever, and if that ever changes you will be told here first.

What exactly do I get after paying?

Unlimited calls with no daily limit, private rooms with a password, and full stats and charts.

What happens when my free 15 minutes run out?

You can keep chatting and browsing; calling opens again tomorrow, or right away on a paid plan.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Pay manually with bKash or Nagad inside the app — send money, submit your number and TrxID, and your plan activates when confirmed. Switch or cancel anytime.

Is it audio or video?

Both — audio and video calls are included on every plan.

Are my calls recorded or listened to?

No. Calls and chats are end-to-end encrypted; the admin sees numbers like minutes and user counts — never your words.

Who is Talk for?

Anyone who wants to practise spoken English out loud, every day — especially university students.
